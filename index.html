Résumé

Hi there! I'm Sandeep, and I like building things, whether that means creating something new or making an existing system easier to use. I'm a technology and delivery leader with close to twenty years of experience modernizing platforms for large, regulated organizations.

I take pride in making sense of complex systems, and I have a sharp eye for the small process gaps that eventually turn into big outages. I enjoy working at the intersection of engineering and delivery, where good architecture meets steady, dependable execution.

I build AI products, including RAG based applications and conversational AI systems, and I lead technical strategy for large scale, mission critical platforms in highly regulated environments. I care about pairing hands on technical depth with the kind of delivery discipline that keeps big systems calm under pressure.
