About

Close to the architecture.
Accountable for the outcome.

I've spent my career where a system going down is a regulatory event, not an inconvenience. For nearly fourteen years at Wells Fargo I set the technical strategy and architectural guardrails for a $2.5T+ AUM finance ecosystem — redesigning it for COSO compliance, building its disaster-recovery discipline, and making its data audit-ready.

My decisions span architecture, budget, roadmap, and stakeholder alignment. I've made the build-vs-buy case to a CTO, rebuilt the support model that keeps finance close running, and restructured teams so each module has an owner. The through-line is turning technical depth into business outcomes that auditors, executives, and engineers can all verify.

Since mid-2026 I've been building independent GenAI products — a RAG conversational platform in production, an agentic content workflow, and an AI knowledge platform — applying regulated-industry rigor to a field that badly needs it.

How I Work

Three principles, earned the hard way.

Evaluate before committing

The cheapest mistake is the one measured early. Cost models and evaluation harnesses come before budget is locked.

Proof: $4M → $1.2M TCO case · RAG go/no-go

Compliance is architecture

Audit-readiness bolted on at the end is brittle. Build classification, validation, and access control in — make the compliant path the default.

Proof: COSO-aligned retention · 52 feeds moving to Apigee

Reliability is engineered

Checkpoint restart, resilient reprocessing, runbooks, and rehearsed failover — failure paths designed, not improvised.

Proof: 99.2% → 99.8% availability · 3–5h DR
