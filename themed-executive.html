Platform modernization

Modernized how core finance systems connect and compute

My teams replaced a third-party intermediary with a direct Bloomberg integration, added a Python computation layer fed by Kafka, and automated daily reconciliation across 142 models that had been checked by hand in Excel.

Reliability and resilience

Made production support and recovery predictable

I built a dedicated L2 support model with runbooks, SLAs, clear ownership and escalation paths, cutting MTTR by 30 to 60%. In parallel, disaster recovery became a rehearsed routine, with failover coordinated across 8 teams twice a year.

  • 99.8%Availability, up from 99.2%
  • 3 to 5hVerified recovery

Governance and compliance

Built compliance into the architecture, not around it

I designed COSO-aligned data retention that enforces Fed and bank-mandated retention windows, with file-receipt validation and access controls. I also led the move of 52 downstream feeds to governed Apigee APIs ahead of a bank-wide mandate.

  • 8 yrsAuditable data trail

Organization and delivery

I restructured a single team spread across four modules into pods with their own backlogs and release rhythm, owned hiring and performance, mentored developers and architects, and stood up a cloud-modernization team on a standardized CI/CD framework.

  • 2×Release frequency

How I Work

Three principles, earned the hard way.

Swipe for more

Evaluate before committing

The cheapest mistake is the one measured early. Cost models and evaluation harnesses come before budget is locked.

Proven by $4M → $1.2M TCO case · RAG go/no-go

Compliance is architecture

Audit-readiness bolted on at the end is brittle. Build classification, validation, and access control in from the start, so the compliant path is the default.

Proven by COSO-aligned retention · 52 feeds moving to Apigee

Reliability is engineered

Checkpoint restart, resilient reprocessing, runbooks, and rehearsed failover. Failure paths are designed, not improvised.

Proven by 99.2% → 99.8% availability · 3 to 5h DR
